## 1. The Open Commons

**1.1 What the Commons is.** The "Commons" is the database of *non-personal* cycling information
contributed by users and the community: climbs, points of interest, bike-friendly stays, road
conditions, and area and road geometry, for example. It does **not** include your personal data
(your identity, history, fitness metrics, or movements), which is private and
processed in accordance with the GDPR and our Privacy Policy.

**1.2 How we license the Commons (outbound).** We publish the Commons openly so that anyone,
including commercial users, may use, share, and build on it for free:
- **Data** is licensed under the **Open Database License v1.0 (ODbL)** (contents under DbCL 1.0).
- **Media** (photos, video) is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.
- **Code**, the platform software itself, is free software under the **GNU Affero General Public
  License v3** (`AGPL-3.0-only`, see [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE)). Anyone may run it, study it,
  change it, and pass it on. Section 13 of that licence adds one thing to the usual copyleft deal:
  whoever runs a modified version as a network service must offer its users the Corresponding
  Source of the version they are using. That duty is ours as much as anyone's: the source of the
  version behind this service is published at
  <https://github.com/cycling-commons/cycling-commons>.
- **UI translations** are part of the software and are licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only** too. That
  holds for the developer-shipped locale YAML in git and for the proposals and approved overlays

Use is subject to the conditions of those licences: attribution, share-alike, keep-open, and for
the code, passing on the source.

**1.3 Your contribution (inbound grant).** When you contribute non-personal data, media, UI
translations, or code to the Commons, you grant the Commons steward (BikeCoders, or the independent
foundation that succeeds it) and the public a **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free
licence** to use, reproduce, adapt, publish, and sublicense that contribution as part of the

**1.4 Your warranty.** You confirm that you have the right to contribute what you submit and that

to share, and translations and code you wrote. **Never** data, images, wording, or code copied,

platform).

**1.5 What is not in the Commons.** Your personal and private data (1.1) is never published as part
of the Commons. Third-party or partner content (for example a closed accommodation scheme's exact
host addresses) is not part of the Commons; where we surface such options we link out to the
provider rather than republishing their data. The Cycling Commons name, logo, and wordmark are not
in the Commons either: they stay with the steward, and the trademark policy in
[`../TRADEMARK.md`](../TRADEMARK.md) says what you may do with them without asking.

**1.6 Our commitment.** We will keep the Commons under open, share-alike licences, and the
software that serves it under the AGPL. We will not re-close either one or place it behind a
paywall. The Commons is intended to be handed to an independent foundation that holds it in trust
for the public (see Governance).

**1.7 Privacy & GDPR.** We adhere to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): we collect
only the personal data we need, process it lawfully and only as described in our Privacy Policy, and
never publish it as part of the Commons. You can exercise your GDPR rights (access, rectification,
erasure, and the rest) by contacting info@cyclingcommons.org.
